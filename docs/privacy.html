Hopstone - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Short version: Hopstone does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app stores

Your progress (cleared levels, stars, points, unlocked pegs and boards, settings) is saved only on your own device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is gone.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No advertising and no ad networks
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location or microphone

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase that unlocks the full game is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Children

The app contains no ads and collects no data from anyone, including children.
